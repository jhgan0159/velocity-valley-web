💡 About Velocity Valley

Why "Velocity Valley"?

The name Velocity Valley represents the dynamic and exciting journey of learning physics. "Velocity" symbolizes motion, progress, and the core principles of mechanics, while "Valley" represents a digital hub where students and educators explore the physical world through interactive tools.

Meet the Founder

Hello! I am Gan Jun Heng, the founder of Velocity Valley Education Simulation Games, a passionate Physics educator and alumni of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) specializing in Physics Education.

By integrating modern web technologies, Velocity Valley helps students visualize and master complex physics experiments beyond rote memorization.
