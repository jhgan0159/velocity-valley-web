About Velocity Valley

Why "Velocity Valley"?

The name Velocity Valley represents the dynamic and exciting journey of learning physics. "Velocity" symbolizes motion, progress, and the core principles of mechanics, while "Valley" represents a gathering place a digital hub where students and educators can come together to explore the wonders of the physical world through interactive tools.

Meet the Founder

Hello! I am Gan Jun Heng, the founder of Velocity Valley Education Simulation Games, a passionate Physics educator and currently a student at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) specializing in Physics Education.

My mission is to break away from traditional rote memorization. By integrating modern web technologies and Constructivist learning theories, I aim to create digital tools that help students truly visualize and understand complex physics concepts.
